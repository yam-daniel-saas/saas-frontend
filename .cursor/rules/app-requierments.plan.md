**Structure:**

1. **Project Context** - Business problem, target audience, and goals
2. **Data Schemas** - User, Employee, Service, Appointment models with all fields
3. **Feature Specifications** - Detailed requirements for each major feature:

- Auth flow (Splash, Login, Signup, OTP)
- Appointments Calendar (day view, modals, filtering)
- Customer Management (CRUD, groups, search)
- Employee Management (schedules, services, roles)
- Services Management (categories, availability, scheduling rules)
- Business Statistics (graphs, filters, time ranges)
- Gallery Management (portfolio, business photos)
- Work Hours Management (shifts, holidays, vacations, time-off)
- Waiting List (card-based UI, date ranges)
- Settings/Menu (profile, business details, notifications)

4. **UI/UX Guidelines** - Design principles, color schemes, light/dark mode
5. **Navigation Structure** - Burger menu + optional tab bar
6. **Technical Stack** - React Native, Expo Router, NativeWind/Tailwind
7. **Development Priorities** - White-label support, professional aesthetic, dual platform support

**Key Details to Include:**

- User types: Business Owner (admin), Employee, Customer
- Appointment statuses: Pending, Completed
- Customer groups: VIP, Regular, Family
- Employee roles: Admin, View-only
- Service scheduling constraints (day/time specific availability)
- SMS integration points (notifications, reminders)
- Calendar features (holidays, vacations, breaks, color coding)
- Statistics timeframes (day, week, month, 6 months, year)

**Format:**

- Use clear markdown sections
- Include specific field names and data types where applicable
- Reference competitors (Calmark, Fresha, EasyTor, Tormahir) for context
- Note that this is the admin/business owner side (customer side will be separate)
- Emphasize professional, modern aesthetic (not childish or minimal)
- Include reminders about dual app strategy (white-label vs single platform)
